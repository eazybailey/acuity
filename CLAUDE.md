# acuity — Equals Results backend + PWA

Next.js 15 (App Router) on Vercel. `lib/acuity.ts` is the Acuity API v1 client (server-only, keys from env). `app/api/gate0/` is the Gate 0 test route.


## Reporting (org rule)

This repo is one project of Eazy Bailey Associates; the rules are in `ORG.md` of the `eazybailey/associates` repo. The lead session rewrites `docs/SITREP.md` at every stopping point and commits it; the first three lines (`updated`, `state`, `next`) are machine-read by HQ — keep the format. The brief is `docs/BRIEF.md`; propose changes in SITREP, never edit the brief. Anything needing Eazy goes in SITREP under "Needs Eazy", recommendation first; never contact anyone outside EBA directly. IC subagents live in `.claude/agents/` and are briefed by the lead only.

## Autonomy

Allowed without asking: code, tests, PRs, preview deploys, docs, research. Not allowed without a logged decision: spending money, production deploys, production data or schema, external contact, scope changes.
